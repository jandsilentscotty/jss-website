I kept a training spreadsheet for [Number] years. It worked, right up until it did not — three
taps to open, pinch-zoom to find today's row, and a keyboard that covered the cell I was trying
to fill in. Between sets, that is three taps too many.

## The problem, stated plainly

Every training app I tried made the same trade. Either it was fast to log and forgot everything
the moment you closed it, or it remembered everything and took thirty seconds to record one set.

What I wanted was narrow:

- Show me what I lifted last time, **before** I load the bar
- Let me log a set without taking my phone out of my bag
- Add up the volume so I do not have to

That is the whole product. [App Name] does those three things and tries very hard not to do a
fourth.

## Who it is not for

It is not a coaching app, it does not have a social feed, and it will not tell you what to train.
If you want a program written for you, there are better tools. If you already know what you are
doing and want the logging to disappear, keep reading this blog.
